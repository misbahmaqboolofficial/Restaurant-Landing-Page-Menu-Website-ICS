Heritage & The Culinary Vision

L'Ambroisie was founded with a singular, clear vision: to establish a space where coffee craftsmanship and fine dining live in perfect harmony. Our name, referring to "Ambrosia" (the nectar of the Greek Gods), represents our commitment to creating heavenly, meticulously balanced dining experiences.

We believe that luxury lies in simplicity. Our bistro interior features raw concrete columns paired with brushed copper fixtures and warm oak wood tables. This intentional design creates an environment that triggers calm and contemplation—the perfect mood to savour our gourmet offerings.

Craft Coffee Bar

Sourcing Micro-Lot Coffee

The journey of every cup of coffee at L'Ambroisie begins months before it reaches your table. We collaborate directly with independent farm cooperatives in Tolima (Colombia) and Yirgacheffe (Ethiopia), paying well above standard market premiums to ensure fair practices and exceptional quality crops.

Our in-house head roaster treats coffee as an art, roasting smaller 10kg batches on our vintage roaster. By controlling temperature profiles down to the single degree, we reveal the unique floral, fruity, and nutty characteristics inherent to each origin.
